Instructions & Scoring Rubric

How to Use This Tracker

  1. Daily Log: Each day, enter your date, week type, and rate yourself 1-10 on 5 dimensions. Colors change automatically (red → amber → green).
  2. Weekly Summary: On Sundays, your weekly averages calculate automatically and appear as a pentagon radar chart showing your "wellness shape."
  3. Track Patterns: Use the chart to spot trends. A lopsided pentagon (e.g., high focus but low motivation) highlights imbalances.

Scoring Rubric

Attribute Low (1-3) Medium (4-6) High (7-10)
Mental Resilience Overwhelmed, unfocused, struggling with decisions Managing okay, some mental fatigue Sharp, clear-headed, handling challenges well
Physical Energy Exhausted, low stamina, sluggish Average energy, getting by Energetic, well-rested, good stamina
Emotional Balance Irritable, anxious, emotionally volatile Stable but some mood fluctuations Calm, content, emotionally regulated
Low Stress Chaotic, high pressure, reactive (1=chaos) Moderate stress, manageable Calm, in control, proactive (10=calm)
Productivity Procrastinating, blocked, unproductive Making progress, some friction Focused, efficient, shipping

Special Notes

Low Stress is Inverted: Unlike the other 4 dimensions, Low Stress reads backwards. 10 = calm day (good), 1 = chaos day (bad). This ensures all 5 scores point in the same direction on the radar chart—bigger pentagon = better week.

Motivation as Career Signal: Your Productivity scores can serve as your career signal. Track them over months to identify when you're in flow vs. hitting burnout.
